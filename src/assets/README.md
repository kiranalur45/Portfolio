# Project Custom Assets Guide

Place your custom media files in these folders to personalize the website:

- `public/resume.pdf` or `src/assets/docs/resume.pdf` — Your downloadable PDF resume.
- `src/assets/images/profile.jpg` — Professional engineer portrait photo.
- `src/assets/certificates/` — Images/PDFs of your FPGA, Embedded, and Edge AI certificates.
- `src/assets/projects/` — Screenshots or hardware diagrams for Women Safety System, Air Mouse, and FPGA Moving Object Detection.
